What matters, who owns it, what happens next, and what proves closure.

This repository supplies the interface and action contract. Connect your own private policy engine, identity provider, storage and workers behind the adapter boundary.

Adapter and safety boundary

The shell validates and displays records. It does not grant authority, send messages, move money, publish, merge code or call external tools.

Consequential execution belongs behind an authenticated adapter that evaluates identity, authority, policy, evidence, duplication, suppression and stop events.
