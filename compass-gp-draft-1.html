Compass + GP = one governed care journey

One patient journey. Different authorised views.

Compass handles the patient flow, evidence, follow-up and receipts. The GP layer gives a clinician the same journey through a clinical workspace — without allowing the system to make the clinical decision for them.

The operating rule

AI can organise, summarise and expose missing evidence. A qualified clinician owns the clinical opinion.

AI work
Prepare chronology · identify missing information · draft non-clinical explanations · maintain evidence trail
Human decide

Before the appointment

Compass prepares the evidence pack and tells the patient what is still needed.

  • Reason for contact
  • Relevant history
  • Medication confirmation
  • Patient questions

During the appointment

The clinician sees the same record, with provenance and gaps visible.

  • No hidden AI conclusion
  • Missing evidence stays visible
  • Clinical notes remain clinician-owned

After the appointment

The patient sees what was decided, what happens next and what remains unresolved.

  • Follow-up actions
  • Results/referrals outstanding
  • Readable receipt of what happened
The platform does not form the clinical opinion.It lays out the evidence, chronology and missing inputs. A qualified clinician interprets it, decides what matters and signs their own decision.

Evidence & governance

A task is not “done” because an AI produced an answer. Draft 1 shows the contract around the work.

1 · RequestWhat did the patient or clinician ask for?
2 · EvidenceWhat information was used, and where did it come from?
3 · AI workOrganise, summarise, prepare — within an allowed scope.
4 · ChecksMissing evidence, policy limits and safety conditions remain visible.
5 · Human decisionThe authorised clinician owns the clinical decision.
6 · ReceiptRecord what was decided, by whom, when and what happened next.
